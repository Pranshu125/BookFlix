Privacy Policy & Terms

Zero data collection • 100% Client-side storage • Open Access Aggregator

1. Absolute Privacy & Zero Tracking

BookFlix does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal credentials. We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data. All reading bookmarks, favorites, and history are kept strictly on your local browser (LocalStorage and IndexedDB).

2. Public Domain & Open Access Aggregation

BookFlix is an indexer and client reader interface. We aggregate open-access metadata, public domain books, and open digital repositories including Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive, Open Library, LibriVox, and public community feeds.

3. DMCA & Takedown Requests

BookFlix respects copyright and intellectual property. BookFlix does not host infringing files on proprietary storage; all documents are streamed or indexed from upstream third-party sources. If you are a copyright owner and wish to request removal of an index link, please submit a notice via our in-app Contact & Feedback Hub under "DMCA / Content Inquiry" for prompt review.

4. PWA Caching & Offline Storage

When installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), BookFlix caches interface assets locally on your device for instant offline launch. You can clear this anytime through browser settings.

From Firebase Console > Project Settings > General > Your apps > Web app config:
How to get your config in 60 seconds:
  1. Open console.firebase.google.com and click Create a new Firebase project (name it BookFlix).
  2. Under Authentication > Sign-in method, enable Google (and optionally Email/Password).
  3. Click Project Settings (gear icon) > General > scroll to Your apps and click the Web </> icon to register the web app.
  4. Copy the firebaseConfig code snippet and paste it into the box above.
  5. Under Firestore Database, click Create database (choose Test mode for immediate access).
